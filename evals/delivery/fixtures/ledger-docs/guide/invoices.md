# Invoices

An invoice bills a recipient for goods or services in the recipient's currency.

## Creating an invoice

1. Open **Invoices** and choose **New invoice**.
2. Pick the recipient. Their currency and payment terms fill in on their own.
3. Add one line per item: description, quantity and unit price.
4. Choose **Save draft**. A draft can be changed until it is sent.

## Sending an invoice

Choose **Send**. The invoice gets its number, is locked against edits, and is emailed as a PDF. The due date is the send date plus the recipient's payment terms.

## Correcting a sent invoice

A sent invoice is never edited. Issue a [credit note](credit-notes.md) for the wrong amount and send a new invoice.
