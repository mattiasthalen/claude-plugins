# Glossary

The words we use for the order book, one entry each.

order
: One customer's purchase as recorded in the book, identified by its id.

customer
: The person or company an order is billed to, written the way they want it on the invoice.

total
: What the customer owes for an order, in kronor, always with two decimals.

placed on
: The day the order was received, not the day it ships.
