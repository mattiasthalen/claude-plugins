# design-and-build

`/design-and-build:design-and-build <pitch>` runs a design interview in plan mode, gap-checks it, gets a plan carrying the brief approved, then captures ADRs, builds through a subagent, reviews and opens a draft PR.

Depends on `design` (Tobias Karlsson's [claude-design-skills](https://github.com/the-exodus/claude-design-skills)), listed in this marketplace and installed alongside.
